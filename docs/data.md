# Corpus policy

The corpus is restricted to public documents from the Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social and other official authorities. A source is eligible only after its official URL, title, date, and version are confirmed and entered in the manifest. Downloaded files are kept unchanged and checked with SHA-256.

Personal or patient information is prohibited. This includes clinical notes, prescriptions, WhatsApp messages, names, identifiers, and private documents. Evaluation questions must be written from public regulation text and contain no real-person details.

The [data layer guide](https://github.com/RafaGM1108/colombia-health-regulations-rag/blob/main/data/README.md) lists local outputs. No regulation documents have been collected at this stage.
